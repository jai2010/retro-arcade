Rules

Finish three laps of the winding oval ahead of two rivals. The gold checkered line marks each lap; drifting past the teal track edges slows the car down. Boost fuel is limited and refills when you ease off.

Controls

Drag left or right on the board to steer, drag up or tap to boost. Keyboard: ← / → or A / D steer, ↑ / W and Space boost, ↓ / S brake, P pause, R restart.
