Rules

A rigid chassis rides on two sprung wheels. Engine torque pushes you up the hills; keep the car balanced, carry momentum through the dips and land flat. Fuel runs out as you drive — grab cans to refill. Rocks end the run, and stars add bonus metres.

Touch

Hold the right half to accelerate and the left half to brake or reverse. Tap to hop over rocks; when the car flips over, tap to flip it upright. Fuel cans refill, stars add bonus metres.
