Rules

You ride up the road while cars and rival riders come down at you from the top. Stay in a clear lane and the distance keeps adding to your score. Touching traffic ends the run; kicking a rival in a neighbouring lane earns 50 bonus points.

Controls

Swipe left or right, or tap to the side of your bike, to change lanes. Tap the bike to kick nearby rivals. On desktop, arrow keys or A/D steer, Space kicks, P pauses and R restarts.
