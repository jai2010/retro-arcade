How to play

Tap a square to reveal it. Numbers show how many of the eight neighbours are mined. Empty squares open a whole region. Your first tap is always safe. Clear every safe square to win.

Long-press a square (or right-click) to plant a flag. Desktop: arrows move, Enter reveals, F flags, P pauses, R restarts.
