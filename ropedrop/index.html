Rules

A gold candy hangs on a teal rope. Cut a rope segment to free it, let it swing and bounce off the obstacles, then land it in the coral dish. Cream stars are a bonus.

Touch

Tap a rope segment to cut it. If the candy settles away from the dish, tap to try the level again. Use Pause and New game above.
