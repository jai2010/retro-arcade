Rules

Pot all six numbered balls to clear the table. Every ball is worth 100; finishing in fewer shots earns a bigger bonus. A dropped cue ball goes back on the spot.

Controls

Press on the cue ball, pull back to set direction and power, then release. On a desktop, arrow keys aim and set power, Space shoots. Strike off-centre for spin.
