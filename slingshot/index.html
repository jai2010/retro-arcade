Rules

Pull the bird back and let go. Topple every block on the platform with three birds. Your score counts the blocks you knock down, plus a bonus for birds you have left.

Controls

Drag the bird back and release to fire. On a desktop, use Up/Down to set the angle, Left/Right for power and Space to launch. Hit the stack low to topple it.
