How it works

Your counter-missile bursts at the tapped point. Any incoming missile inside the blast is destroyed — and its wreckage can set off a chain reaction.

Cities & ammo

Save all six cities. Ammo replenishes each wave, and a clean wave pays a bonus. Lose every city and the game is over.
