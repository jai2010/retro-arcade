Safe landing

Both feet must be on the flat pad, the lander close to level, and the descent slow. Touch down anywhere else — or too fast — and it's a crash.

Fuel & score

Thrust burns fuel. The score rewards a centred touchdown and the fuel you have left. On a phone, drag up to thrust and left or right to steer.
