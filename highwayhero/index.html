Rules

Traffic rolls toward you in four lanes. Distance earns points; slipping past a car in the next lane earns a near-miss bonus. Touch a car and the run is over.

Sprint

Swipe left or right to change lanes and tap the field for a short sprint. Sprint doubles your distance rate while it lasts, then needs a moment to recover.
